**Send:** 3–5 working days before Session 1. Optional one-line reminder 48 hours before.

**Why so short:** participants get a lot of email. In the pilot, almost nobody read the pre-session messages, so this one does one job: the ask. Don't describe or show the bad-slide exercise. It only works if it's a surprise.

**If nobody reads it:** plan as if they didn't. Bring the bad-slide pack; anyone without a slide uses one from it.

### The message

**Subject:** Teaching That Lands, [date]: bring one bad slide

Hi [name / all],

Looking forward to Session 1, *Death by PowerPoint?*

**When:** [day, date, time] (90 minutes)\
**Where:** [room, building]

**One ask:** if you can, bring a slide you think is bad. One of your own, or one you've sat through. On a laptop, a phone or on paper is fine.

The redesign is on paper, so you don't need a computer. If you'd rather work in PowerPoint and you're comfortable with it, bring a laptop.

No slide, or no time? Come anyway. We'll have some in the room.

[Your name]

### Reminder (48 hours before)

> Teaching That Lands, [day] [time], [room]. If you can, bring a slide you think is bad. If not, just come.
