**Send:** the day after Session 2. Optional one-line reminder 48 hours before Session 3.

**Why so short:** as before, the message reads back the card from the room and nothing else. Don't preview Session 3's content. The end of the session depends on it being new.

**If nobody reads it:** Session 3 opens with 2–3 people running what they made on the group. Ask for volunteers on the day. If nobody made anything, run one of your own and ask "what got in the way?"

### The message

**Subject:** Teaching That Lands, [date]: bring what you made

Hi [name / all],

Thanks for Session 2. Here's the ask you wrote on your card:

> "Before next week I will spend 30–40 minutes playing with Menti or Kahoot and make one active learning thing for my session, and bring it."

**Bring:** the Menti or Kahoot you made, with the link or code ready. A few of you will run yours on the group, so a short one is perfect.

**When:** [day, date, time] (90 minutes)\
**Where:** [room, building]

Session 3 is the last one: *Did It Stick?*

Didn't get round to it? Come anyway. Bring the session you've been working on.

[Your name]

### Reminder (48 hours before)

> Teaching That Lands, [day] [time], [room]. Bring the Menti or Kahoot you made. Didn't manage it? Come anyway.
