## Overview: before you start

You only need to do a few things on a computer in this programme. They're all below, one step at a time.

**New to some of this?** That's fine, and more common than you'd think. Tell the facilitator: you'll be paired with someone, and everything can be done on paper too. For help beyond the programme, your organisation's IT service desk or digital skills team can help.

On a work computer, PowerPoint may open in your web browser instead of as an app. The steps are almost the same.

### Session 1: working on a slide (laptop route)

The Session 1 redesign is on paper. These steps are only for people who choose to do it in PowerPoint.

**Open a PowerPoint file**\
Double-click the file. Or open PowerPoint, then **File → Open**, and find the file.

**Save a copy before you change anything**\
**File → Save a Copy** (or **Save As**). Add "redesign" to the name, so the original stays safe.

**Select slides**\
Slides are listed down the left-hand side.
- *One slide:* click it.
- *Several in a row:* click the first, hold **Shift**, click the last.
- *Several, not in a row:* hold **Ctrl** (Windows) or **Cmd** (Mac) and click each one.

**Copy a slide to try something new**\
Right-click the slide on the left → **Duplicate Slide**.

**Undo a mistake**\
**Ctrl + Z** (Windows) or **Cmd + Z** (Mac). Press it again to go back further.

**Check accessibility**\
**Review** tab → **Check Accessibility**. Work through the list it shows.

### Session 2's ask: make one active learning thing

**Open a web browser**\
On most NHS computers that's **Microsoft Edge**. Click the address bar at the top, type the address, press **Enter**.

**Mentimeter** (polls, word clouds, quizzes)
1. Go to **mentimeter.com** and **sign up** (free).
2. Create a new presentation.
3. Choose a slide type, such as a word cloud or multiple choice, and type your question.
4. Click **Share** and copy the link, or note the joining code. People join at **menti.com**.

**Kahoot** (quiz games)
1. Go to **kahoot.com** and **sign up** (free).
2. Click **Create**, then make a new kahoot.
3. Add a question and its answers. Save it.
4. Click **Share** and copy the link.

Buttons move around as the sites update. If you can't find something, look for **Share**, **Create** or a **+**.

**Keep your link**\
Email it to yourself, so you can open it next week on any computer.

### Session 3: show what you made

1. Open the email with your link and click it.
2. **Mentimeter:** click **Present**. People join at **menti.com** with the code.
3. **Kahoot:** click **Start**. People join at **kahoot.it** with the game PIN.

No link, or it won't open? Describe what you made. That counts.
