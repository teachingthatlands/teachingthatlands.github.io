**Rule:** if a row is still blank when you send the first invitation, you haven't finished planning.

## 1. Aim and success

| Question | Your answer |
|---|---|
| **What should change?** Which teaching, by whom? Not "better teaching": better how? | |
| **What counts as success?** Set the bar now, before anyone has attended. | |
| **Scope:** which staff groups, which sites, how many cohorts? | |
| **Who owns it?** Which person or team is accountable for delivery and follow-up? | |
| **Do the suggested outcomes fit your learners?** The [five outcomes](/facilitator/setup-and-justify) are written for the content. Check them against who is coming and what they need; reword or replace what doesn't fit. | |
| **Is academic credit wanted?** If so, agree it with the institution now: see [Academic credit](/facilitator/setup-and-justify). | |
| **Is this cohort part of something bigger?** If your organisation is also reviewing how it plans teaching in general, the [Teaching Design Check](/facilitator/design-check) is the place to start. | |

## 2. Stakeholders

| Who | What they need from you | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Sponsor** | The [Briefing for Decision-Makers](/facilitator/setup-and-justify) and a clear ask: protected time | Before you announce it |
| **Participants' line managers** | A short note: what this is, the time it takes, and that you'd like them to ask participants what changed | Before invitations |
| **Participants** | Honest framing: three 90-minute sessions, small tasks in between, and one session of their own to rebuild | Invitation |
| **Facilitator** | The session guides, and a named colleague to debrief with | Before Session 1 |
| **L&D or CPD lead** | Attendance record for portfolios and revalidation | After Session 3 |

**The stakeholder most often missed is the line manager.** Change at work depends on whether people are supported and held to account afterwards, not just on the training (Level 3 of the [Kirkpatrick Model](https://www.kirkpatrickpartners.com/the-kirkpatrick-model/); see [Kirkpatrick](/theories/did-the-training-work)). Brief managers before the programme, not after.

## 3. Participants

| Question | Your answer |
|---|---|
| **Who, and how many?** Aim for 8–12. It works from 6 to 20. | |
| **How are they chosen?** Volunteers, nominated or required? Required attendance changes the mood in the room; tell the facilitator. Volunteers may flatter the results. | |
| **What will they bring?** One session they teach, or would like to teach. | |
| **Access and equity:** dyslexia, colour vision, English as an additional language, shift patterns, caring responsibilities. Plan for these before inviting. | |
| **Minimum numbers:** if you can't confirm 6 two weeks before Session 1, postpone. | |

## 4. Logistics

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Dates** | Three sessions, 90 minutes each, one week apart. Same day and time each week if you can. |
| **Room** | Screen, whiteboard, chairs that move. Book all three now. |
| **Facilitator and cover** | Both named. The cover knows where the materials are. |
| **Kit** | Ask cards, pens, the Redesign Worksheet printed double-sided for each person, the bad-slide pack. See the [Technical Brief](/facilitator/technical-brief). |
| **Tools** | Free Mentimeter and Kahoot accounts, set up and tested. Check local IG first. |
| **Comms** | Who sends invitations, reminders and the 30- and 90-day checks? |

## 5. Evaluation

Decide this before Session 1, not after Session 3. Use the design in the [Evaluation Toolkit](/facilitator/evaluation-toolkit), including its governance steps, and name the person who will send the 30- and 90-day checks. If that's you and you might move on, hand it to a colleague now.

## 6. Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Participants pulled back to clinical work | High | High | Protected time agreed in writing with line managers before invitations |
| Facilitator unavailable | Medium | High | Named cover with all materials |
| People drop out after Session 1 | Medium | High | Reminder two days before; the ask card is read back at the start of each session |
| Required attendees arrive disengaged | Medium | Medium | Facilitator acknowledges it openly at the start |
| Tech fails | High | Low | Every tool has a low-tech fallback |
| No follow-up data | High | High | Name the follow-up owner and put the dates in their diary today |
| Nothing changes back at work | High | High | Brief line managers; each participant agrees with a named person (often their manager) who will hold them to it at 30 days, and the support they need |

Add your own rows. The risks you skip here are the ones that surprise you.

## Sign-off

- [ ] Sponsor has read the Briefing and agreed protected time
- [ ] All three dates and the room are booked
- [ ] Line managers are briefed
- [ ] Facilitator and cover are named and have the session guides
- [ ] Mentimeter and Kahoot are set up and tested, with fallbacks ready
- [ ] The 30- and 90-day follow-up owner is named and the dates are in their diary
