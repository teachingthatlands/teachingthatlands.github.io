**Send:** the day after Session 1, while the ask is fresh. Optional one-line reminder 48 hours before Session 2.

**Why so short:** the ask was written on a card and photographed in the room. This message only reads it back, word for word. Don't add new tasks or theory.

**If nobody reads it:** Session 2 opens with "show us your slides" anyway. Anyone who didn't do it gets asked, kindly, "what got in the way?" Their answer is useful. Have the bad-slide pack to hand.

### The message

**Subject:** Teaching That Lands, [date]: bring your slides

Hi [name / all],

Thanks for Session 1. Here's the ask you wrote on your card:

> "Before next week I will identify and redesign one or two slides (my own, or an example I find) and bring them."

**Bring:** the slides you redesigned or found, on a laptop, phone or paper. A before-and-after is ideal.

**When:** [day, date, time] (90 minutes)\
**Where:** [room, building]

Session 2 is *Active Beats Passive*.

Didn't get round to it? Come anyway. We'll start from wherever you are.

[Your name]

### Reminder (48 hours before)

> Teaching That Lands, [day] [time], [room]. Bring the slides you redesigned or found. Didn't manage it? Come anyway.
